ABOUT CCMS

Making civic services more responsive

CCMS is a digital platform where citizens can submit civic complaints and monitor their progress until resolution.

📝

Report

Submit a complaint with category, description, location and optional evidence.

🔎

Track

Use your unique complaint ID to see the latest status and progress.

🏛️

Manage

Authorities can review complaints, assign work and update status.

✅

Resolve

Citizens can see when an issue reaches the resolved stage.
